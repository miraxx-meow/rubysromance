Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Welcome to rubysromance.com. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website or subscribe to updates.

1. Information Collected

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when subscribing to our newsletter or release updates via our signup form. This includes:

  • First Name
  • Email Address

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used strictly for author-related communication, including:

  • Sending news regarding book releases (such as Lily of the Valley), pre-orders, and special offers.
  • Distributing subscriber-exclusive content, cover reveals, teasers, or bonus material.
  • Responding to direct inquiries or messages you send.

We will never sell, rent, trade, or share your email address or personal details with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to securely process form submissions and manage website performance:

  • Snapit Forms: Handles form submission processing. You can review Snapit's privacy commitments on their official website.
  • Cloudflare / GitHub Pages: Provide domain security, HTTPS encryption, and website hosting.

4. Unsubscribing & Managing Your Data

You can opt out of receiving email updates at any time by clicking the Unsubscribe link included at the bottom of any email newsletter, or by contacting us directly to request the deletion of your email address from our records.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This site is designed to respect visitor privacy and does not use invasive tracking cookies or sale-targeted advertising trackers.

6. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at readrubysromance.com.
